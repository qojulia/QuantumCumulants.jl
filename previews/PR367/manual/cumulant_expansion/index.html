Cumulant expansion

The Heisenberg equation for a product of $n$ operators generally involves products of $n+1$ operators, so the equations for averages never close on their own. The cumulant expansion cuts this tower: it assumes that all joint cumulants above a chosen order vanish and rewrites every higher moment through lower ones. The theory page derives the expansion, and Convergence and validity discusses when the truncation can be trusted.

Order of an average

The order of an average is the number of operator factors it contains. It decides which averages the expansion rewrites and which it keeps.

using QuantumCumulants

h = FockSpace(:cavity) ⊗ NLevelSpace(:atom, (:g, :e))
@qnumbers a::Destroy(h)
σ(i, j) = Transition(h, :σ, i, j)

get_order(average(a' * a * σ(:e, :e)))
3
QuantumCumulants.get_order — Function
get_order(expr)

Highest moment order appearing in expr, the order used to decide whether a term is expanded by cumulant_expansion. Numbers have order 0, a bare operator order 1, and a product the number of its operator factors.

Examples

julia> h = FockSpace(:a) ⊗ FockSpace(:b);

julia> @qnumbers a::Destroy(h, 1) b::Destroy(h, 2);

julia> get_order(a)
1

julia> get_order(a * b)
2

julia> get_order(1)
0

Joint cumulants

The joint cumulant of a product measures the correlation that is not already contained in its factors. For three operators it is the third moment minus every way of splitting it into smaller moments.

cumulant(a' * a * σ(:e, :e))

\[ \begin{equation} \langle a^{\dagger}a{\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle - \langle {\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a^{\dagger}a \rangle - \langle a{\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle - \langle a^{\dagger}{\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a \rangle + 2 ~ \langle a \rangle ~ \langle {\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle \end{equation} \]

Setting this cumulant to zero yields the second-order expansion of the third moment.

cumulant_expansion(average(a' * a * σ(:e, :e)), 2)

\[ \begin{equation} \langle {\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a^{\dagger}a \rangle + \langle a{\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle + \langle a^{\dagger}{\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a \rangle - 2 ~ \langle a \rangle ~ \langle {\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle ~ \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle \end{equation} \]

QuantumCumulants.cumulant — Function
cumulant(op, n=get_order(op))

The n-th joint cumulant of op (either an operator or an average), the signed sum over partitions of its operator factors into n blocks. The result is raw and unsimplified; apply SymbolicUtils.simplify for a canonical form.

Examples

julia> h = FockSpace(:a) ⊗ FockSpace(:b);

julia> @qnumbers a::Destroy(h, 1) b::Destroy(h, 2);

julia> cumulant(a * b)
⟨a * b⟩ - ⟨b⟩*⟨a⟩

julia> cumulant(a * b, 1)
⟨a * b⟩

julia> cumulant(a * b, 3)
0

Expanding equations

cumulant_expansion acts on single averages, on arbitrary expressions of averages, and on a whole MeanfieldEquations. The order keyword of meanfield applies it during the derivation, which is the usual route. Expanding afterwards is useful to compare several orders on the same raw equations.

@variables Δ::Real g::Real κ::Real
H = Δ * a' * a + g * (a' * σ(:g, :e) + a * σ(:e, :g))
eqs = meanfield([a' * σ(:g, :e)], H, [a]; rates = [κ])
cumulant_expansion(eqs, 1)

\[ \begin{aligned} \partial_{t} \langle a^{\dagger}{\sigma}^{{12}} \rangle &= \langle {\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle g i - \langle a \rangle \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle g i + \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle \langle {\sigma}^{{12}} \rangle \left( - \frac{1}{2} \kappa + i \Delta \right) + 2 \langle a \rangle \langle {\sigma}^{{22}} \rangle \langle a^{\dagger} \rangle g i \end{aligned} \]

A hybrid system may need different orders on different subspaces, e.g. a classical-like cavity coupled to strongly correlated atoms. A vector order sets one cap per Hilbert subspace, and mix_choice decides the cap of a term that spans several.

QuantumCumulants.cumulant_expansion — Function
cumulant_expansion(expr, order; mix_choice=maximum)

Expand every average in expr whose order exceeds order into lower-order moments via the joint-cumulant identity, neglecting the joint cumulant above order. order is an Int (one cap for all subspaces) or a Vector{Int} (a cap per Hilbert subspace, combined by mix_choice on mixed terms). The output is raw; apply SymbolicUtils.simplify for a canonical form.

See also: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cumulant#Joint_cumulants

Examples

julia> h = FockSpace(:a) ⊗ FockSpace(:b);

julia> @qnumbers a::Destroy(h, 1) b::Destroy(h, 2);

julia> cumulant_expansion(average(a * b), 1)
⟨b⟩*⟨a⟩
source
cumulant_expansion(eqs::MeanfieldEquations, order)

Cumulant-expand every RHS of eqs to order, returning a new MeanfieldEquations of the same shape with that order stored. The system's mix_choice (held in eqs.graph.sys) drives the mixed-order truncation. Noise systems take their order through meanfield, not here.
